## ChromeWeb Introduction

- A website to modify web experiences using **mods made with CSS, HTML, and JavaScript**.
- Turn your browser into a canvas — inject any code, override any style.
- Built entirely with the open web: CSS, JavaScript, and HTML.

---

## Hero Section

ChromeWeb is a platform where you can discover, share, and run **public mods** —  
either as **Bookmarklets** (JavaScript snippets) or **CSS files** — to reshape any website to your advantage.

Whether it’s a dark theme for Wikipedia, a custom UI for Discord, or a floating music player on every page, ChromeWeb makes it possible.

---

## Why ChromeWeb?

- **Audio anywhere** – Play audio from your favorite music providers or spin up a `.wav` file right inside any website.
- **Special variants** – Launch ChromeCord on Discord, giving you the feel and look of Discord Nitro without paying a cent.
- **Plugin system** – Custom plugins for all the websites you use, shared and installed with one click.
- **Cross‑platform** – Use it as a browser extension, a desktop executable, a bookmarklet, or even an iOS Shortcut.

---

## Features

- **Universal CSS injection** – Replace any site’s stylesheet instantly.
- **JavaScript & HTML injection** – Add functionality, widgets, or custom elements.
- **Mod Editor** – Write, save, and export your own mods from a built‑in dashboard.
- **Marketplace** – Browse community‑made mods, both free and premium (Stripe/PayPal).
- **ChromeCord** – A built‑in Discord enhancer with fake Nitro perks, emoji everywhere, and profile effects.
- **Floating audio player** – Load local/remote audio and control playback on any page.
- **Rich Presence bridge** – (Desktop) Connect to Discord’s local RPC for “Listening to…” status.
- **Liquid Glass UI** – Modern, frosted‑glass design components for your own creations.
- **Sandboxed Bookmarklets** – Quick theming without any extension.
- **iOS integration** – Apply CSS themes via Apple Shortcuts.
